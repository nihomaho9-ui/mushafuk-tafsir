Which tafsir should I open first?

  • Short: Aysar and al-Muyassar.
  • Clear and complete: al-Saadi.
  • Reports: Ibn Kathir.
  • The long collection: al-Tabari.

The address is the surah and the verse. Ayat al-Kursi is 2/255 in every book.

What is tafsir.mushafuk.online?

This address is where the Arabic tafsirs are read verse by verse. Each explained verse has its own page.

How many books are here?

There are 17 Arabic tafsirs.

How many pages are here?

These books have 84,708 verse pages. A complete tafsir has a page for each of the 6,236 verses. Some books only contain the verses the author wrote on, so they have fewer pages.

How do I open a page?

Choose the book, the surah, and the verse. An address looks like /shawkani/2/255/.
